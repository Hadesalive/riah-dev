## Platform

web

## Users
Decision-makers who commission ICT work in Sierra Leone and West Africa, weighted equally (confirmed by the user, 2026-10-07):
- IT directors and procurement officers in ministries and public institutions.
- Programme managers at NGOs and health programmes (DHIS2, RapidPro, donor-funded).
- Banks, fintechs and businesses needing payments, networks, licensing and support.
- Hotels and venues (Waka TV, campus Wi-Fi), secondary.
Their job on the site: understand within seconds what RIAH does, judge whether RIAH can be trusted with mission-critical systems, and request a technical consultation.

## Product Purpose
riah.dev is the marketing site for RIAH SL Limited, an enterprise ICT services and engineering firm. Success is a visitor who understands the offer and sends a consultation request (or, for development partners, a sponsorship inquiry).

## Positioning
One accountable team across layers that are normally split between suppliers: network and firewalls, Linux servers and databases, application review and deployment, RapidPro SMS joined to Monime mobile money, DHIS2 health informatics, and the licences (Zoho, NGFW, Waka TV). Built for low-bandwidth, unreliable-power conditions.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from referrals, procurement searches and partner introductions, often on mobile and on limited connections. Many are not engineers (procurement, programme managers), so the offer must be readable without technical knowledge; engineers on the buying side need enough specifics to trust it.

## Product Principles
1. Say plainly what RIAH does before saying anything clever.
2. Show the systems at work, so non-engineers can see what they would get.
3. Credibility comes from specifics (named technologies, real conditions), never from invented proof.
4. Every sector should find itself on the site within one scroll.
